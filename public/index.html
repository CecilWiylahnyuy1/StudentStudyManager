STUDENT STUDY MANAGER

Track your tasks, organize your notes, and build a study streak that actually sticks.

How it works

Student Study Manager brings everything a student needs to stay on top of their work into one simple place. You add your assignments as tasks with due dates, jot down notes right after each study session while the material is still fresh, and lay out a weekly timetable so studying has a set time and place not something squeezed in whenever there's a spare moment. As you complete tasks and follow through on your study sessions, the app rewards that consistency with a daily streak, giving you a small, visible reason to keep showing up even on days when motivation is low. Over time, this turns studying from a scattered, last-minute scramble into a steady habit one that reduces stress before deadlines, improves how well material is actually retained (since notes and quick self-quizzes reinforce what was just studied), and builds the kind of discipline that pays off well beyond any single course or exam.

What it does

  • 📌 Tasks: Keep track of assignments and deadlines, and mark them done as you complete them.
  • 📝 Notes: Save notes by subject, especially right after a study session capturing what you actually learned while it's fresh.
  • 🗓️ Timetable: Plan out your study sessions for each day of the week, so studying becomes a routine instead of an afterthought.
  • 🔥 Streaks: Stay motivated with daily streaks complete a task or finish a study session with notes, and your streak grows. Just like Duolingo, missing a day breaks it, but you get a second chance to recover it.
  • 🧠 Quizzes: After writing notes on a study session, answer a few quick questions generated from your own notes to check what stuck.

Why it exists

Most students don't struggle from lack of ambition they struggle from lack of structure. Student Study Manager gives you just enough structure to stay consistent, without turning studying into extra admin work.
